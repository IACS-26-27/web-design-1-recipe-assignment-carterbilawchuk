Premium Grilled Cheese

Ingredients:

  • Sourdough Bread
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Tomato
  • Ham
  • Salt
  • Smoked Paprika
  • Chopped Onion
  • Red Pepper Flakes

Info:

  • Preparation Time:

    30 mins

  • Serving Size:

    1 sandwich

  • Complexity:

    very simple

Instructions

Prep ingredients

Slice the sourdough bread into ½-inch thick slices. Slice the tomato and cheddar cheese. Dice the onion if not already chopped.

Dry the tomato

Place tomato slices on a paper towel and blot the excess moisture. This keeps the sandwich from getting soggy.

Butter the bread

Spread a thin layer of butter (or brush with oil) on one side of each slice of bread. This will be the outside of the sandwich.

Season the cheese side

On the unbuttered side of one slice, lay down a layer of cheddar cheese. Sprinkle it with a pinch of salt, smoked paprika, and red pepper flakes.

Add the ham

Place the ham slices on top of the seasoned cheese.

Add the onion and tomato

Scatter the chopped onion over the ham, then lay the tomato slices on top. Sprinkle the tomato lightly with salt.

Top with more cheese

Add a second layer of cheddar cheese over the tomato. This helps hold the sandwich together.

Close the sandwich

Place the second slice of bread on top, buttered side facing out.

Heat the pan

Set a skillet over medium-low heat. Let it come up to temperature for a minute or two.

Grill the first side

Place the sandwich in the pan. Cook for 3–4 minutes, apply pressure to the top, until the bottom is golden brown and the cheese begins to soften.

Flip carefully

Use a spatula to flip the sandwich. Cook for another 3–4 minutes until the second side is golden and the cheese is fully melted.

Check for completion

The bread should be crisp and browned, and the cheese should be gooey. If the bread browns too fast, lower the heat and cover the pan briefly to help the cheese melt.

Rest and slice

Transfer the sandwich to a cutting board and let it rest for 1 minute. Cut in half and serve hot.

Enjoy!
